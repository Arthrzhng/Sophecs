title: The machine heart
source:
  author: Zhuangzi
  work: Heaven and Earth
  section: "12"

body: >-
  The motion says that handing your daily choices to an assistant costs you
  something, even when its choices are better. Chapter 12 of the Zhuangzi
  names a candidate for what the something is, and locates it somewhere
  unexpected.


  Where there are ingenious contrivances, the chapter says, there are sure
  to be subtle doings, and a scheming mind. Then the line that does the
  work: when there is a scheming mind in the breast, its pure simplicity is
  impaired. The damage is not in the contrivance and not in the outcome it
  produces. It is in what the habit of using it makes of the person using
  it.


  That is a different complaint from the one about losing a skill. A
  recommender does not merely choose for you. It trains you to expect the
  shape of its choosing, to frame what you want in terms it can take as
  input, to treat the question of what to do as a lookup. The disposition
  arrives whether or not any particular recommendation was good.


  Two cautions. Chapter 12 is an Outer Chapter and should not be put in the
  mouth of the historical Zhuang Zhou. And readers disagree about how far it
  goes: whether it rejects machinery itself, or only the technologies that
  produce dependency and a calculating mind.
