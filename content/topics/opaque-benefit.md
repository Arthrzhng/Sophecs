title: "The opaque benefit"
motion: "An AI system that reliably reduces suffering should be deployed even where the people it decides about cannot be told why."
stances:
  stoicism: "Deploy it if the people responsible judged the evidence well; the mechanism is an external, and a sound judgement does not become unsound because the reasons cannot be passed on."
  utilitarianism: "Deploy it, because the suffering it prevents is real and an explanation was only ever a means to that end."
  virtue-ethics: "Do not deploy it, because justice to the person a decision lands on includes owing them an answer, and a practice of withholding one forms the wrong character."
  confucian: "Do not deploy it: an office that decides about people owes them an account of its decisions, and one nobody can explain leaves them nothing to hold the office to."
  ubuntu: "Do not deploy it until the people it decides about have a say, because a benefit no one can explain to them shuts them out of a decision about their own lives."
  buddhist: "Deploy it only if those running it keep checking whom it still harms and answer anyone it fails, because an opacity that hides its harm cannot be squared with non-harm."
